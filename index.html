About MyJyotish

MyJyotish is a Vedic astrology guide for everyone. It answers your questions about marriage, career, money, health, property, children, travel, good dates and kundli matching in simple words. Every answer is worked out only from the rules of classical astrology books, explained in MyJyotish's own words, and the book and chapter for each rule are shown.

Developed by Amitava Chandra

Mr. Amitava Chandra is an electrical engineer from BIT Mesra. He spent 29 years at Tata Steel, then served as General Manager of the Jaypee Group's thermal power plant in Prayagraj and as Vice President at Kanpur Fertilizers & Chemicals Ltd (Jaypee Group). After a lifetime of engineering, he built MyJyotish with the same discipline: rules first, facts checked, and everything explained clearly, so that the wisdom of Jyotish reaches ordinary people honestly. The same approach is behind his app Gita Sarathi.

Our promise

  • ✅ No made-up predictions: every point comes from a book rule, with its source.
  • ✅ Honest and kind: good and difficult points are both stated plainly.
  • ✅ No fear: MyJyotish never predicts death, and it does not show yogas linked to death, crime or disease.
  • ✅ Your data stays on your device: birth details are saved only in your own browser.

Astrology is guidance, not certainty. Results depend on an exact birth time and place. For health, legal or money decisions, please also consult a qualified professional.
